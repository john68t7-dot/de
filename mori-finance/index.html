Pitch

Problem

Holders of Solana tokens have few good ways to earn on what they own. Lending yields have compressed, and options venues like Deribit only list majors like BTC and ETH. Long-tail tokens like PENGU have no options market at all. So holders either leave tokens idle, lend them for low single-digit yield, or sell and try to buy back lower. Treasuries, DAOs and funds face the same problem at size. They want to earn on their holdings and set the prices at which they’d sell or buy, without dumping tokens on the market.

Solution

Mori is building the options layer for Solana. It's a DeFi frontend with a CeFi backend: Mori custodies user funds and places trades on users' behalf through its own OTC account with institutional market makers. Today that's an institutional options market maker backed by Kraken, and the system is built to add more. That means no smart contract risk and no reliance on thin onchain orderbooks, the two problems that sank earlier onchain options. It also lets Mori list long-tail tokens quickly and at size.

Covered calls (live): Holders pick a token, a sell price and an expiry, and get USDC premium upfront. If the price stays below the strike, they keep their tokens and the premium. If it goes above, they sell at the price they chose.

Cash-secured puts (coming soon): Holders set a price they'd happily buy a token at and get USDC premium upfront. If the price drops below it, they buy at that price.

dOTC desk (coming soon): Anyone can request options on any altcoin on Solana, HyperEVM, Ethereum or Base. Mori sources a quote from institutional market makers, so a token doesn't need an existing options market to get one. Because these are bespoke quotes, dOTC trades have higher minimum sizes and may have per-token maximums. First tokens: GRASS, JUP, MET and ORCA.

Option builder codes (coming soon): Trading apps and protocols can plug into Mori's RFQ and offer options to their own users, earning a fee on every trade they route. Each integration adds distribution without Mori having to acquire those users itself. Companies interested in integrating include ImperialPerps and Orca.

Market

Mori serves crypto holders who want more yield than lending pays, starting with individuals and expanding to treasuries, DAOs and funds holding Solana tokens. The market is growing fast: onchain options now do about $1.44B in monthly notional, and premiums hit all-time highs in 2026 (Castle Labs). The closest comparable is Rysk, which runs the same model for HYPE holders. It grew from $50M to $182M in monthly volume in just five months. If Mori matches that on Solana, our 12% cut of premium would bring in roughly $2.8M a year. The dOTC desk and builder codes extend Mori from individual holders to funds, treasuries and every app on Solana that wants to offer options.

Risks

  • Custody and counterparty

    The CeFi backend removes smart contract risk but adds custody and counterparty risk. Mori custodies user funds and trades through its own OTC account, so users rely on Mori as custodian, and Mori carries counterparty exposure to the market makers it trades with.

  • Market maker concentration

    All of Mori's trades currently go through one market maker.
